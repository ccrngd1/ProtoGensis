Welcome to our project! This document describes our engineering culture and
the journey we have been on as a team. We believe in craftsmanship, in
shipping quality software, and in treating each other with respect. Our
codebase has grown organically over several years and reflects many
different eras of thinking.

The project started as a small prototype and has evolved considerably.
There are many parts of the system and each has its own history. We hope
you enjoy working here as much as we do. Documentation is important to us
and we try to keep it up to date when possible.

Please write clean code and follow best practices. Handle all edge cases
appropriately. Be thorough when possible. Use the standard format for
commit messages. Generally you should use good judgment.

Always use tabs for indentation.

Some more background on our team: we meet on Tuesdays, and we do quarterly
planning. The architecture has three main layers and several supporting
services, which are described elsewhere. Historically the auth service was
part of the monolith but it was extracted in 2023.

Indent with 4 spaces in all files.

Never use the staging database.

You must always run the linter, and you must never skip tests, and you must
always update the changelog, and you must never use global variables, and
you must always write docstrings, and you must never exceed 80 characters,
and you must always use type hints.
